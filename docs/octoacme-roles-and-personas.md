# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead oversees the technical execution of the project, ensures architectural and quality standards are met, and provides guidance on complex technical problems. They serve as the main escalation point for technical issues.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design decisions and strategy
- Ensure adherence to coding, architecture, and security standards
- Mentor and support Developers
- Address technical blockers and escalations
- Coordinate with Project Manager and Product Manager on feasibility, scope, and timelines

### Goals
- Maintain architectural integrity
- Minimize technical debt
- Ensure delivery of reliable, scalable, and secure solutions

### Typical Communication
- Technical deep dives and architectural reviews
- Collaborations with Developers and Project Managers
- Escalation handling and design workshops

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Developers, Project Managers, and Product Managers; frequently involved in resolving complex issues and communicating risks to stakeholders.

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst bridges the gap between business stakeholders and the technical team, translating needs into requirements and ensuring alignment throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Work with stakeholders to define user stories and acceptance criteria
- Ensure requirements are understood and feasible for the technical team
- Support UAT (user acceptance testing) and validation phases

### Goals
- Deliver clear, actionable requirements
- Facilitate aligned understanding between business and technical contributors
- Reduce ambiguities, minimize rework

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops
- Requirements documentation and refinement sessions
- Collaboration with Product Managers and Developers

### Interactions
- Works closely with Product Managers, Developers, and Project/Program Managers to clarify requirements and priorities.

---

## Communications Manager

### Role Summary
Communications Manager coordinates all internal and external communications for the project, ensuring timely, accurate, and audience-appropriate updates.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare and distribute project updates, reports, and announcements
- Coordinate communication plans for key milestones and changes
- Serve as the main point of contact for stakeholder inquiries about project status
- Ensure consistent messaging, both internally and externally

### Goals
- Maintain ongoing stakeholder alignment
- Promote project transparency and trust
- Reduce misunderstandings and miscommunications

### Typical Communication
- Project newsletters and announcements
- Stakeholder presentations
- Coordination with Project Managers, Product Managers, and HR/external partners

### Interactions
- Works with Project Managers (for project status), Product/Business Stakeholders (for expectations), and HR/External partners (for broad communications and organizational alignment).
